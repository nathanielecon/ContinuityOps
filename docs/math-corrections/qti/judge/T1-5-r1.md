  Part 1 Question 2 | 7 | presentation/material/mattext (XML line 139)
    found:  At 6:00 PM, the temperature is \(-3.75^\circ\)C. It drops
            $2.4^\circ\(C, then drops $1.85^\circ\)C. What is the temperature
            at midnight? Round to two decimals only if needed.
    why:    Delimiters are interleaved, not merely mixed. Reading left to right
            the spans are: `\(-3.75^\circ\)` (well formed), then a bare
            `$2.4^\circ` with no partner, then `\(C, then drops $1.85^\circ\)`.
            The `\(` before "C," opens a math span that does not close until the
            `\)` after `1.85^\circ`, so MathJax typesets the prose "C, then
            drops $1.85^\circ" as mathematics while the actual quantity
            `$2.4^\circ` falls outside the span and renders as the literal
            characters `$2.4^\circ`. The `\(` and `\)` counts are each 2, so a
            naive balance check passes -- the defect is in the ordering, and
            both numbers a student needs (2.4 and 1.85) are the ones corrupted.
    fix:    Replace the first `<p>` of the stem with:
            At 6:00 PM, the temperature is \(-3.75^\circ\)C. It drops
            \(2.4^\circ\)C, then drops \(1.85^\circ\)C. What is the temperature
            at midnight? Round to two decimals only if needed.
            (HTML-escaped in the XML exactly as the surrounding markup is.)
